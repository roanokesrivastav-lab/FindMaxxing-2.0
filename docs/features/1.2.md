# 1.2 — Six-interest thumbnail picker

Priority: Must. Branch `codex/founder-implementation-handoff-2026-11-11`, starting commit `e726e8d`
(batch 00 record). Status: **IMPLEMENTED-LOCAL.** Not independently verified; no device run.

## Contract

- **Objective:** the first onboarding step asks "What would you go out for?" with six recognizable
  interests as rows (thumbnail, label, visible checkmark), the rest under "More interests", a visible
  Skip and a forward button that says "Continue to home city". No minimum. Whatever the picker accepts
  is exactly what the first map filters by.
- **Allowed files:** `src/components/onboarding/`, onboarding save/validation
  (`packages/shared/src/taxonomy.ts`, `validation.ts`), the Explore page's tag parsing, the Explore
  filter sheet's limit, targeted tests and browser scripts.
- **Prerequisites verified:** batch 00 baseline green; 1.1 primitives and tokens present; pilot flags
  unchanged ([00-baseline](00-baseline.md) §3).
- **Out of scope:** the first-arrival state on the map (1.3); the People step's social context line
  (1.4, Later); camera, selection or filter restoration (2.2, Later); native onboarding; any migration.
- **Preflight:** not required. UI and validation only, no schema, permission or payment change.

## Changes

- **One limit, end to end.** `MAX_INTERESTS = 12` and `parseInterestTags()` now live in
  `packages/shared/src/taxonomy.ts`. They are used by the profile schema, the onboarding link, the
  Explore page and the discovery query schema. Before this, the picker accepted 12 and three places
  cut the list to 8 (`WelcomeFlow.tsx`, `src/app/(explore)/page.tsx`, the `tags` parser in
  `validation.ts`), so up to four chosen interests never reached the map.
- **The limit is said, not applied silently.** The helper text reads "up to 12, or none". At 12, the
  remaining options are marked unavailable (`aria-disabled`, dimmed, described by the limit line) and a
  status line says "That's 12, the most the map can use at once. Remove one to pick another." Tapping
  an unavailable option changes nothing and emphasises that line. Before, the thirteenth tap did
  nothing with no explanation.
- **Picker.** `WelcomeFlow.tsx` step 1: six rows from `FEATURED_INTEREST_SLUGS` (Pickup games, Coffee,
  Cheap eats, Outdoors, Nightlife, Studying), each a `role="checkbox"` button with a thumbnail, a
  one-line description and a check mark that changes shape and fill. "More interests" is a disclosure
  (`aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) over the other 18, shown as chips with a check when selected and
  a "(n selected)" count on the button. It starts open when a saved choice lives there.
- **Thumbnails.** `InterestThumb` shows a photo when one is configured and loads, otherwise a tinted
  glyph tile in the `PlaceGlyph` style. **No interest photos are bundled**, so every row shows the
  glyph today (see limits).
- **Skip and forward.** "Skip" leaves the step without saving and discards unsaved taps, so the People
  step cannot match on interests that were never saved. "Continue to home city" saves the selection,
  including an empty one. The top-right "Skip setup" is unchanged.
- **Step buttons** wrap at large text sizes instead of running off the screen (found by the 200% check).
- **Explore filter sheet.** The same limit applies when adding interests there, with "Up to 12
  interests at a time. Remove one to add another." Without this, a thirteenth filter would have been
  dropped by the query parser without notice.
- **Tests and scripts:** `tests/onboarding.test.ts` (new), `scripts/e2e-onboarding.mjs` (new,
  `npm run e2e:onboarding`, added to CI after the a11y step), and the golden-journey onboarding step in
  `scripts/e2e.mjs` updated for the new picker.

Migrations: none. Dependencies: none. Flags: none added (onboarding is not behind a pilot flag today).
Demo and Supabase: the picker and link are shared code; tag filtering is covered on both sides below.

## Choices recorded (reversible; not founder decisions)

- **Which six.** Chosen for the Columbus pilot and for having seeded results: Pickup games, Coffee,
  Cheap eats, Outdoors, Nightlife, Studying. Changing them is one array in `taxonomy.ts` plus copy in
  `src/lib/onboarding/interests.ts`.
- **The limit stays 12** (the number the profile already allowed) rather than dropping to 8. The map
  now honours all 12, which is the first of the two options the acceptance criterion offers, and the
  limit is also stated at selection.
- **Thumbnail source.** The seed has no photos and public photos must be team-uploaded or permissioned,
  so no image was invented or fetched. Rows are built to take one per interest.

## Adversarial review

- **Silent truncation elsewhere.** Searched for other caps on interest lists. Found and fixed the
  Explore filter sheet (above). Left alone, and outside this ticket: the trip planner takes at most 8
  interests (`src/app/api/trip-plan/route.ts`, `TripPlanner.tsx`), and a place or event can carry at
  most 8 tags (`tagsSchema`). Neither is first-map filtering; the trip planner's cut is recorded here
  as a known silent truncation for whoever owns trips.
- **Tampered links.** `?tags=` with unknown, duplicated or more than 12 slugs is reduced to known
  slugs, de-duplicated and capped by the one parser, on the page and in the API (tested).
- **Bypassing the picker.** Sending 13 interests straight to the save action is refused with "Pick up
  to 12 interests" and nothing is saved (tested).
- **Unsaved taps leaking into later steps.** Skip now resets the local selection, so People
  suggestions are ordered by saved interests only.
- **Hidden selections.** A saved interest outside the first six opens "More interests" on return, so
  no checked item is hidden behind a closed control (tested in markup and in the browser).
- **Private data.** The link carries interest slugs only, the same values the profile already stores
  and the map already accepted in the URL. No new data leaves the profile.
