(stub: people opt in, per activity and area, to hear when a nearby session still needs players
("2 more needed", from 4.3). Off by default; respects quiet hours, blocks, the daily push cap and mutes
(4.6 pipeline); deduped per session; never RSVPs. "Spec must define radius, frequency cap and what
'nearby' means without exposing anyone's location"). Lane B1. Flag: `pilotEvents`. Migration **0058**.

## Spec (written first; recommended option taken for every open point)

### Contract

- **Opt-in, off by default.** A person turns alerts on, picks one or more **activities** (event
  categories, e.g. Pickup sports, Fitness) and an **area**.
- **Area without exposing location:** the area is a **coarse cell**: a latitude/longitude rounded to
  **0.01°** (about 1.1 km north–south) plus a **radius** of 1, 3, 5 or 10 km. Only the rounded cell is
  stored (rounded in the database, whatever the client sends), it is readable only by its owner (RLS),
  it is never shown to anyone else, and matching runs server-side. The default area is the pilot city
  centre; "Use my location" rounds the browser position before saving.
- **"Still needs players"** = a session that is free, published, visible to the person, not ended, with
  a minimum (`min_attendance`), **more needed > 0**, decision still **open**, its decision deadline (if
  any) still ahead, and starting within the next **48 hours**. Not sent for the person's own events, or
  events they are going to, waiting for or offered.
- **Nearby** = the session's point lies within the radius of the cell centre (equirectangular distance,
  accurate to well under 1% at pilot scale).
- **Frequency cap:** at most **one alert per session per person** (dedupe key `need_players:<event>`)
  and at most **3 need-players alerts per person per rolling 24 hours**. Push delivery then applies the
  existing quiet hours and daily push cap (5); blocks (the host blocked by the person) and mutes apply
  through `notify()`.
- **Never RSVPs:** sending an alert writes only a notification.
- New notification type **`need_players`**, pushed by default only to people who opted in (they are
  the only recipients).
- Runs in the existing 15-minute notifications job (`send_need_player_alerts`, service-only).

### Acceptance

1. Off by default; a person reads and writes only their own settings (RLS); the stored cell is rounded.
2. A matching session sends one alert; a second run sends none (dedupe); a fourth matching session in 24 h
   is not sent (cap).
3. No alert for: other categories, outside the radius, met minimum, decided, past deadline, more than
   48 h away, priced, own event, already going/waitlisted, hidden from the person, host blocked, type muted.
4. Settings page (pilotEvents) with loading, empty and error states, 390×844, keyboard only.
5. Demo twin at parity; RLS as anon, owner, another user, moderator.
