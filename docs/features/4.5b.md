# 4.5b Series page

Built 2026-11-05 under [beta-build-rules.md](../beta-build-rules.md). Ticket: roadmap-80.md §4, M4 "4.5b",
batch 4.5b of the approved [4.0 spec](4.0.md). Lane B1. Flag: `pilotEvents`. **No migration.**

## Contract

- A permanent page per series, `/series/[id]` (Local editorial): schedule in plain words ("Saturdays at
  9:00 AM EST"), venue, description, **next confirmed session** (with its Readiness row), the rest of the
  upcoming sessions, **past sessions**, **hosts**, the **newcomer note**, and **Follow** (updates only,
  never an RSVP, and the page says so).
- Host tools on the page: the owner adds a backup host by username or removes one (immediate); hosts
  tick **who came** on recent past sessions (`event_attendees.attended`, never inferred).
- Each occurrence page links to its series ("Part of a weekly series: …").
- Off (`pilotEvents` off): the page 404s; nothing else changes.

## Choices recorded (recommended option taken)

- **"Next confirmed session"** = the next upcoming occurrence the host confirmed, or one with no
  minimum to wait for. If none qualifies, the heading says "Next session" with "Not confirmed yet:
  it happens if enough people sign up by the host's deadline."
- **1.5 rule extended as recorded in 1.5:** occurrences of a series you follow count as "events you
  usually go to" (row note "From <series>, which you follow"), and so do later occurrences of a series
  you went to. Following still creates no attendee or participant row.
- **Past sessions** shows the last 8 ended, non-cancelled occurrences with how many signed up;
  attendance marking is offered on the last 3 to hosts only.
- **Demo seed:** one series, "Saturday morning pickleball" (host hana_host, backup marcus, followed by
  pat_participant, Saturdays 09:00 New York, min 4, decide 12 h before), with its first three Saturday
  occurrences seeded under stable ids in both the SQL seed and the demo store (pat going to the first).
  The generator adds the fourth week. Sign in as `host@example.com` for host tools.
